Professional Summary

I'm a Workday Integration Developer with hands-on enterprise delivery experience from my role as a Software Developer at Tata Consultancy Services in Bangalore. There, I worked on Apple's Workday implementation in the USA, building Core Connector (CCW), EIB, Document Transformation and Workday Studio integrations, and supporting them through UAT, pre-cutover, post-cutover and go-live. After go-live, I delivered monthly sprint enhancements and fixes across PECI, WECI and multi-document transformation integrations. I also built advanced custom reports and migrated integrations between Implementation, Sandbox and Production tenants using OX 2.0. My technical base is XSLT 2.0/3.0, XML and XPath, and I work directly with clients to turn business requirements into integration designs. I hold a BTech in Computer Science and Engineering and am completing an MSc in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence at Sheffield Hallam University, researching lightweight deep learning for IoT anomaly detection. What sets me apart is real Workday delivery experience combined with growing data and AI skills, recognised with a TCS Star of the Month award and a special award from the client.

Mission

To build reliable, well-documented Workday integrations that keep people data accurate, secure and on time, so HR, payroll and finance teams can trust every record that moves between systems.

Vision
